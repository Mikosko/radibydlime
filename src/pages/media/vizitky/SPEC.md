# Business-card print workspace

The rose contact side includes an 18 mm SVG QR anchored at the bottom-right below its four values, linking to the person's permanent digital card. Contact details and the QR destination resolve `contact-jitka` from the [contacts collection](../../../content/contacts/SPEC.md). QR encoding runs only at build time, with a warm ivory quiet zone independent of the selected paper texture and color. A separate watercolor wash softens the surrounding corner without covering the code. Keep the QR inside the safe area and test a physical print before production.

`/media/vizitky/jitka-rajnoskova/v1/` (natural) and `/media/vizitky/jitka-rajnoskova/v2/` (rose) are separate unlinked, Czech, owner-facing static print pages. The original `/media/vizitky/jitka-rajnoskova/` URL redirects to `v2`. Each page renders only its two card sides and has no design selector. Route assembly resolves the contact and supplies it with the design to [BusinessCardWorkspace](../../../components/business-card-workspace/SPEC.md). SiteLayout uses minimal mode and `noindex, nofollow`; indexing exclusion is not authentication. No credentials or private records belong here. This URL namespace is independent of the external media host and its catalog.

Both sides use the existing brand, local botanical artwork, typography and palette. Owner-confirmed name, shared mailbox, website and phone from the contact record are used. The creative line “Květiny · domov · malé radosti” is a design proposal, not a professional qualification. Default finished size is 90 × 50 mm with 3 mm bleed on each side, a safe inset of 3 mm from trim, and an optional 3 mm corner-radius guide. Checkbox controls independently show safe, trim and rounded guides. They are preview-only and never print.

Printing produces two 106 × 66 mm pages, front then back. Each page has a 5 mm outer margin around the unchanged 96 × 56 mm bleed artwork. Eight black vector crop marks align to the 90 × 50 mm trim boundary, remain outside the bleed with a 1 mm gap, and print regardless of screen-guide toggles. They never appear in the screen preview. The outer margin is white; selected textures and colors apply only to the card. Physical millimetre dimensions and fixed print type sizes must remain independent of screen width. Hide workspace controls, labels, preview guides, navigation and shadows; keep artwork and background colors. The print button waits for fonts and artwork; native browser printing remains available without JavaScript. Instructions request 100% scale, no margins/headers/footers and background graphics. This is not a duplex imposition or certified PDF/X export; confirm requirements with the chosen printer.

Validate noindex, physical PDF page size and page count, both sides, independent guide toggles and their absence in print, mobile preview and the static production build. Contact facts are shared with the digital card through the contacts collection.

An independent checked-by-default “Ořezové značky” checkbox beside the print button controls printed crop marks. When disabled, output returns to two 96 × 56 mm bleed-only pages without outer margins or marks. This works with native browser printing and without JavaScript, using a named print page when marks are enabled; screen guides remain independent.

The transparent-background radio option removes the cream fill from both cards and the printed page. A screen-only checkerboard indicates transparency and never prints; the physical paper supplies the final background. Use full-resolution lossless PNG botanical artwork, including BrandMark’s print-quality mode, rather than its small web derivative.

The two versioned pages share contact facts, guides, dimensions and transparency; transparency removes only the paper fill/texture. The rose page has dusty pink accents, an illustrated craft still life and a decorative contact-side composition. Only the page's front/back pair is emitted or printed, including without JavaScript. All wording remains HTML. The supplied craft PNG retains its small pink painted details as part of the artwork.

Independent keyboard-accessible radio palettes select eleven paper colors (or transparent) and five texture treatments: smooth, fine paper (default), watercolor paper, plant fibers and linen. Circular texture samples show the selected paper color. Colors include the original palette plus pale olive, grey-green and peach from the supplied texture reference. Original cream is the default. Selection applies to both faces and print, works without JavaScript, and does not change physical dimensions. Transparent removes both paper fill and texture; checkerboards remain preview-only. Texture assets are local PNGs blended over the chosen color, with a 65% paper-color veil to keep their grain subtle in samples and on printed cards.

The rose design explicitly layers paper color/texture, an independent transparent dusty-pink watercolor PNG, then illustration and live text/logo. The wash is a permanent part of this design for screen and print, including transparent paper; it has no separate toggle. The natural design remains clean. Guides sit above all design layers and never print.

On the rose card’s back, the enlarged botanical and watercolor extend beyond the left/bottom edges and are clipped at the bleed boundary. Preserve artwork aspect ratios; do not stretch the watercolor to a narrow strip. Contact text remains inside the safe area.

“Skutečný vzhled” is a screen-only presentation of the trimmed card against the unchanged page background with slight rotation and soft shadow. It clips the 3 mm bleed and hides guides without changing their checkbox state. Selecting the rounded-corner guide also previews its 3 mm finish radius in this mode. Print retains the unrotated full bleed pages regardless of this toggle. This is a visual mockup, not a physical-color or paper-stock proof.

Realistic preview applies a thin layered lower paper edge and soft contact/cast shadows to the trimmed card silhouette, not the surrounding surface. These effects are screen-only and do not enter print output.

The experimental “Parciální lak” simulation is currently disabled pending revision and review. Its code is retained behind `enableVarnishPreview = false`; no toggle is rendered, no pointer handlers are attached, and no gloss is applied. It is not a production varnish mask or a physical proof of finish.

The preview toolbar presents guide toggles, realistic view and fullscreen as one horizontal icon row in normal view. Fullscreen contains texture and color controls in a compact left third, with the selected card pair centered in the right two-thirds. Below 900 px these regions stack. The same inputs serve both modes, preserving selections. In fullscreen the toolbar stays fixed outside the scrolling card area: fullscreen and realistic-preview icons at the top-right, guide icons stacked at the viewer's top-left. Accessible names, tooltips, selected states and keyboard focus remain available. Preview windows do not clip card shadows; the card itself still clips artwork to its physical boundary. Where native fullscreen is unavailable, an expanded viewport preview provides the same controls and Escape dismissal. These presentation controls never print.
