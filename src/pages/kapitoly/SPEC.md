# Chapter index

`/kapitoly/` assembles published Project records in canonical newest-first order through the existing query. PageContainer and PageHeader supply shared introductory spacing and typography. The content uses a calm editorial list inspired by the Gallery index: copy on the left, photography on the right, quiet row dividers and small rounded photo corners. Desktop keeps its editorial two-column composition without decorative interruptions between entries.

Each chapter shows its topic, title, summary, canonical author portrait/name, Czech publication date formatted in UTC and a chapter link with a pen-like underline on hover or keyboard focus. The newest chapter gets a quiet label, not a different template or artificial numbering. The hero leads the photo composition; up to two distinct supporting images come from its resolved album, excluding the hero. No photographs are invented or repeated to fill slots. Chapters without an album show only their hero.

Mobile and tablet show photography first in one connected bordered card. Topic, latest label and title overlay the hero with paper-colored text, a dark gradient and text shadow; only the breakpoint-appropriate title is visible. The photo area grows for wrapped titles. Description, canonical author/date and a right-aligned EditorialLink follow below. Mobile has 4px top/side inset around the photos; previews reflow beneath the hero. Desktop retains text left and photos right. All photo links open the Project, not an image viewer. Preserve native links, visible focus, meaningful image alt and one h1. Draft/archive visibility and the empty state follow the Project contract. No extra JavaScript or content model is introduced.
