# Chapter index

`/kapitoly/` assembles published Project records in canonical newest-first order through the existing query. PageContainer and PageHeader supply shared introductory spacing and typography. The content uses a calm editorial list inspired by the Gallery index: copy on the left, photography on the right, quiet row dividers and small rounded photo corners. There are no overlapping card panels or decorative interruptions between entries.

Each chapter shows its topic, title, summary, canonical author portrait/name, Czech publication date formatted in UTC and a chapter link with a pen-like underline on hover or keyboard focus. The newest chapter gets a quiet label, not a different template or artificial numbering. The hero leads the photo composition; up to two distinct supporting images come from its resolved album, excluding the hero. No photographs are invented or repeated to fill slots. Chapters without an album show only their hero.

Mobile reading order is copy then photos; previews reflow beneath the hero. All photo links open the Project, not an image viewer. Preserve native links, visible focus, meaningful image alt and one h1. Draft/archive visibility and the empty state follow the Project contract. No extra JavaScript or content model is introduced.
