# Workshop pages

The listing and static detail routes share [Workshop content](../../content/workshops/SPEC.md). The owner-supplied workshops.png reference informs date labels and practical metadata. Rows follow Kapitoly: text, hosts and enquiry links on the left, shorter photo/date on the right with icon-led facts in three columns on a translucent paper panel over the bottom of the photo. The photo extends behind this panel; grid rows let the metadata grow without clipping, separated by quiet rules. Dates on photos reuse CalendarBadge with Europe/Prague timezone. No stretched paper backgrounds sit behind row content. Past tickets are compact, use muted lightly sepia photographs and an “Ohlédnutí” stamp on the photo, and do not offer reservations. This visibly distinguishes past workshops without relying on color alone. Mobile and tablet rows form one continuous bordered card: photo and practical facts first, then padded text, hosts and enquiry links on a shared light surface, without a gap between image and copy. On mobile and tablet, category and title sit at the bottom of the photo above the facts, with paper-colored text, a dark gradient and text shadow. The description, hosts and booking remain below. Breakpoint-hidden headings ensure only one visible heading per workshop; photo rows grow to accommodate wrapped titles and leave the calendar clear. Desktop retains text on the left and photography on the right. The header reuses the first six published workshop heroes plus two existing editorial photographs at the sides in a compact overlapping scatter of tilted photo prints and a small overlapping paper note. The composition scales down on narrow screens, resembles prints laid on a desk and adds no carousel. The header has no bottom divider. Preserve shared PageHeader typography and PageContainer spacing. No filtering or listing-page scripts. Detail return links use the shared ReturnToList enhancement.

Detail pages show one h1, a large photo, Markdoc description/program/preparation text, and a bounded, subtly bordered practical-information panel. WorkshopFacts and WorkshopBooking keep date/time/duration/capacity and reservation semantics shared with the index. Preparing topics have no fabricated date; full events offer a waiting-list enquiry; ended details contain no reservation action. No fake event galleries, payment controls or unsupported price claims. Workshop navigation remains active for all detail routes. Native links, image alt, visible focus and decorative empty alt follow UI contracts.

Mail drafts include the workshop's stable ID, name, scheduled date, requested party size and visitor name. Availability is not live: a reservation exists only after personal email confirmation. The current dates/counts are simulated at the owner's request. Validate list/detail consistency, lifecycle visibility, invalid capacity, duplicate identity and responsive rendering. The site remains static.

Tickets and details show one or two workshop hosts via WorkshopHosts, resolved from the existing author profiles. Detail pages use a larger host introduction above the narrative.
