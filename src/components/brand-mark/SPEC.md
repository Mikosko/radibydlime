# Brand mark

`BrandMark.astro` owns the reusable live Rádi bydlíme mark: a real semibold serif wordmark, accent heart, and the approved compact botanical illustration anchored above the final `íme` area. It does not own a link, tagline, surrounding estate illustration, or page-specific alignment; the header and footer compose those around it.

The wordmark remains real text. Its words are always left-aligned internally, including when a parent centers the whole logo or surrounding content. The heart and botanical image are decorative and hidden from assistive technology. The default `stacked` variant places the words on separate lines for the header and footer information band; `inline` provides the demonstrated one-line landscape-footer treatment. `size` supports the demonstrated scales, `loading` follows the consuming context, and an optional class lets the parent position the mark without changing its internal relationship.

Print consumers may opt into `printQuality` to embed the full-resolution botanical PNG instead of the 42px web derivative. Display dimensions and brand composition stay the same.
