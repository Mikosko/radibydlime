# Business-card workspace

Shared presentation for the two concrete versioned print pages. Receives a resolved contact record and `design: 'natural' | 'rose'` from route assembly. Does not query content or offer a design selector; it renders only that design's two sides. Shared styling, color/texture controls, fullscreen and print behavior follow the [print-workspace contract](../../pages/media/vizitky/SPEC.md). Keep this component specific to the current card designs rather than making a general design editor.
