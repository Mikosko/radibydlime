# Product variants

Receives authored variant data, product identity/title and enquiry eligibility from the detail page. Native radio controls select one option and reveal its price, optional original price/discount label, and variant-specific email enquiry. Stable product/variant IDs and the selected label and price are included in the email. No runtime fetches, persistence, checkout, stock or voucher redemption logic.

First authored variant is selected initially. Keep keyboard radio navigation, visible focus, and polite price announcements. Without JavaScript all options/prices and their enquiry links remain visible; hide the inactive selector until enhancement. Sold/reserved products do not expose enquiry links. Images remain the shared authored gallery; selection never invents a photo-to-variant relationship.
