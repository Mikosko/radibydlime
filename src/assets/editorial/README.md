# Editorial sample photography

These images support the temporary article presentation and will be replaced or reassigned as real editorial material becomes available.

The six `sample-gallery-*.webp` images were generated with the built-in image tool in September 2026 as fictional restoration photographs. Prompts requested documentary views of a farmhouse courtyard, exposed ceiling beams, an old door and stone threshold, hands applying plaster, reclaimed roof tiles, and a rural workbench. They use natural light and muted earth colors, with no text or logos. They do not document the actual estate.

`sample-author-jan.png` was generated with the built-in image tool in September 2026 as a fictional placeholder portrait, not a likeness of a real person. The prompt requested a candid, natural-light portrait of a friendly Czech man near a rural workshop and old house, with muted olive, cream, wood, and earth colors, realistic texture, space for a circular crop, and no logos, text, watermark, or corporate headshot treatment.
